# HaulHub — Frequently Asked Questions

HaulHub is a marketplace for getting things moved. People who need an item delivered post a request. Drivers who are already making that trip, with spare room in their vehicle, offer to carry it.

**Senders** get cheaper delivery than a courier or moving company. **Drivers** earn money on a journey they were going to make anyway.

## 1. General

### What is HaulHub?
HaulHub is like an online marketplace, but for deliveries instead of buying and selling. Senders post what they need moved and where it needs to go. Drivers heading in that direction can browse those listings, or get matched to them automatically, and offer to carry the item.

### Who is HaulHub for?
- **Senders:** anyone who needs something taken from A to B. That could be furniture you bought online, a parcel for family in another city, household goods, or stock for a small business.
- **Drivers:** anyone with a vehicle and spare space who is already making a trip. That includes everyday drivers, commuters, people relocating, and independent couriers or carriers.

### Why is it cheaper than a courier or moving company?
The driver is already making the trip, so you aren't paying for a dedicated vehicle or a journey made just for your item. You're only paying for the space your item takes up on a trip that was happening anyway.

### Can I be both a sender and a driver?
Yes. One account can post delivery requests and also offer to carry items on trips you're making. _[TBC: whether one account covers both roles or a separate driver profile is needed]_

## 2. For Senders

### How do I request a delivery?
1. Create a listing with:
   - what the item is
   - its approximate size and weight
   - photos
   - the pickup location
   - the drop-off location
   - when it needs to be moved
2. Your listing appears in the public marketplace feed. Drivers going your way may also be matched to it automatically.
3. Review driver offers, chat with drivers in the app, and accept the one that suits you.

### What information should I include in my listing?
The more detail you give, the better your offers will be. Include:
- **Dimensions and weight:** so drivers know it fits their vehicle
- **Photos:** of the item and its packaging
- **Pickup and drop-off details:** stairs, parking, building access codes
- **Whether help with loading is needed:** for example, a heavy sofa may need two people
- **Your timing:** a fixed date, or a flexible window

### How quickly will I find a driver?
It depends on how many drivers are travelling your route. Busy routes between large towns usually get offers quickly. Remote routes may take longer. A flexible delivery window makes a match more likely.

### Can I choose my driver?
Yes. You can see each driver's profile, rating, past deliveries and vehicle details before you accept their offer.

### Do I need to be there at pickup and drop-off?
You, or someone you nominate, must be at pickup to hand the item over. At drop-off, the recipient must be there to receive it and confirm delivery. _[TBC: whether a third-party recipient can be named in the app]_

### How should I pack my item?
Pack it as you would for any courier. Use sturdy boxes, protect fragile items, and seal everything securely. Drivers can refuse an item that is poorly packed or doesn't match the listing.

### What items can't I send?
Prohibited items will include anything illegal, dangerous, hazardous or perishable without suitable transport. The full list is _[TBC]_.

## 3. For Drivers

### How do I start driving with HaulHub?
1. Create an account.
2. Complete identity verification.
3. Add your vehicle details.
4. Post the trips you're planning, or browse listings along your route.

_[TBC: whether a driver's licence and vehicle insurance are checked]_

### Do I need a van or truck?
No. Any vehicle with spare room works: a car, an estate, an SUV, a van or a truck. Only accept items that safely fit your vehicle.

### How do I find jobs?
You can do either or both:
- **Browse** the marketplace feed and filter by route, date and item size.
- **Post your trip** (where you're going, when you leave, and how much space you have) and HaulHub will suggest listings along your route.

### Can I carry items for several senders on one trip?
Yes, as long as everything fits safely and you can meet each sender's pickup and drop-off times.

### Do I have to go out of my way?
No. HaulHub is designed for trips you're already making. You choose which jobs to accept, so only take ones that fit your route.

### How much can I earn?
That depends on the distance, the item's size and weight, and what you and the sender agree. _[TBC: pricing model — see Pricing & Payments]_

### Can I turn down an item at pickup?
Yes, if it doesn't match the listing, is unsafe, is poorly packed, or looks like a prohibited item. Record the reason in the app. _[TBC: how this affects payment and cancellation]_

## 5. Tracking & Delivery

### Can I track my item?
Yes. Once the driver collects your item, you can follow the delivery live on a map in the app.

### How will I know my item has been delivered?
The driver records **proof of delivery** in the app, such as a photo or the recipient's confirmation. You'll get a push or email notification as soon as it's done.

### Can I message my driver or sender?
Yes. Use the in-app chat to arrange pickup times, share access instructions or ask questions. Keep communication in the app so there is a record if a problem comes up.

### Will I get notifications?
Yes. HaulHub sends push and email notifications for:
- new offers
- accepted jobs
- pickup
- delivery
- messages

You can adjust these in your settings.

## 6. Safety & Trust

### How do I know a driver or sender is genuine?
Users complete identity verification before they can send or carry items. Profiles show ratings and reviews from previous deliveries, so you can see someone's track record before agreeing to a job.

### What happens if my item is damaged or lost?
Report it in the app as soon as possible, with photos. The proof-of-delivery record and the chat history will be used to resolve it. _[TBC: damage/loss protection cover and claim limits]_

### Is it safe to meet a stranger for pickup?
Meet at the address on the listing. Share your plans with someone you trust, and keep all communication in the app. If anything feels wrong, you can cancel and report the user.

### How do ratings work?
After each delivery, the sender and the driver rate each other. Consistently low ratings or reports can lead to an account being restricted or suspended.

### How do I report a user or listing?
Use the **Report** option on the profile, listing or chat. Reports are reviewed by the HaulHub team.

## 8. Account & Privacy

### What personal information does HaulHub collect?
HaulHub collects what it needs to run deliveries safely:
- your name and contact details
- identity verification data
- location during active deliveries
- payment information, which is handled by the payment provider

Details are in the HaulHub Data Privacy & Security Policy.

### Is my location tracked all the time?
No. Live location is shared only while a delivery is in progress.

### Who can see my address?
